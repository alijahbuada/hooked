The cocoon

“These little spirit things come out from a dead goat’s mouth. They start to pull threads out of thin air.”

You pulled enough threads to weave one. Sen calls it “a certain something” she can only describe as a cocoon, and is never yet convinced it will be a butterfly and not a moth.

Director’s note. HOOKED is an homage to the flat, tasteless summers of COVID, and to the strange feeling right after you move out of your childhood home. Nothing happens, and that’s the point. Two people chase quick fixes to feel something, then sit at a lake where nothing bites.
